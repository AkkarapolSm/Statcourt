THESIS: Follow the supplied sports landing page structure: oversized wordmark behind an athlete, a quiet introduction, an analytics feature, audience stories, a wide image banner, live information, FAQ, and a strong final invitation.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve StatCourt's crimson, court ink, ice surfaces, Barlow Condensed and Noto Sans Thai. Keep the existing navigation, authentication, API-backed schedules, leader filters, and member tools.

STORY: A Thai basketball visitor sees the sport first, understands how statistics connect to opportunities, and can open live games, player information, or organizer solutions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy stage has a large white STATCOURT wordmark behind an isolated athlete. Thai copy and a crimson action sit at lower left; a compact match-center link sits at lower right. On mobile the athlete has a separate visual region above the readable copy.

FORM: User-selected structure from the attached references, adapted within the established brand. No randomized concept selection is needed. The signature interaction is the analytics metric selector and native FAQ disclosure.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this extension ends with the finish review, the verdict, the home surface handoff in `docs/home-reference-verification.md`, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance. Existing `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and `.impeccable/design.json` are preserved; their inherited tokens remain authoritative.
